OCRFlow now captures as well as reads: take anything on screen, from any app, and get searchable text or one assembled document.

**New**

- **Screenshots from any app.** Press ⌥⇧⌘2, or use the menu bar or the floating button, then choose an area or a window. Recognize the text, save or copy it from the bar beside it. Capturing never brings OCRFlow forward.
- **Long screenshots.** Scroll by hand or let Auto Scroll do it, and watch the joined picture grow as it goes.
- **Queue Screenshots.** Drag out area after area with no shortcut in between, until you press Esc.
- **Record Actions.** Do something once, such as capturing a slide and pressing →, and OCRFlow repeats it a set number of times or until the screen stops changing, and can do so across a list of web addresses. What it captures becomes one document, one long picture or separate pictures. Every recording can be edited step by step.
- **Menu bar.** OCRFlow can stay in the menu bar with no Dock icon.

**Better**

- Results, the list and review show lines joined as written, in plainer wording throughout.
- A page where the layout model finds nothing is still read.

**Permissions.** Screenshots need macOS's Screen Recording permission. Record Actions, Queue Screenshots and Auto Scroll also need Accessibility. See [what stays on your Mac](https://github.com/yb311/OCRFlow/blob/main/docs/PRIVACY.md).

OCRFlow 现在不仅能识别，还能截取：从任何应用截取屏幕内容，得到可搜索的文字，或合成一份文档。

**新增**

- **在任何应用里截图。** 按 ⌥⇧⌘2，或使用菜单栏、浮动按钮，然后选择区域或窗口。在旁边的操作栏里识别文字、保存或拷贝。截图时 OCRFlow 不会跳到前台。
- **长截图。** 手动滚动或使用自动滚动，拼接结果会随之实时变长。
- **队列截图。** 一次又一次拖出区域，中间不用按快捷键，按 Esc 结束。
- **录制操作。** 操作一遍（比如截一张幻灯片再按 →），OCRFlow 就会按设定的次数重复，或一直重复到屏幕不再变化，还可以依次处理一组网址。截到的内容可以合成一份文档、一张长图或多张单独的图片。每个录制都可以逐步编辑。
- **菜单栏。** OCRFlow 可以只留在菜单栏，不显示 Dock 图标。

**改进**

- 结果、列表和复核中的文字按原文接行，界面用词也更直白。
- 版面模型没有找到内容的页面，也会照常识别。

**权限。** 截图需要 macOS 的“录屏”权限。录制操作、队列截图和自动滚动还需要“辅助功能”权限。详见[哪些数据留在你的 Mac 上](https://github.com/yb311/OCRFlow/blob/main/docs/PRIVACY.md)。
